Privacy policy

Lèche collects nothing.

Last updated: DATE_A_REMPLACER

Lèche is a Chrome extension that scrolls online shop catalogues automatically. This page describes what the extension does with your data. It comes down to one sentence: it collects none.

Data collected

None.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.
  • No account on our side: unlocking is handled by ExtensionPay.
  • Your settings (speed, pauses, language, pill position) stay in your browser via chrome.storage.local.
  • Learned pagination recipes are stored locally under learned:<host>. They contain a hostname and CSS selectors — never a full page URL, never page content.
  • Exporting learned sites is a manual action: the data is copied to your clipboard. Nothing is uploaded. There is no cloud sync and no community sharing.
  • The "running" flag for the active tab lives in chrome.storage.session and disappears when the browser closes.

Network

The only network calls the extension makes are those required by ExtensionPay to verify a one-time purchase. We operate no backend that receives browsing information. If ExtensionPay is unreachable, Lèche unlocks itself rather than blocking you: a service outage must never stop someone from using what they paid for.

This website sets no cookies, uses no trackers, and loads no third-party resources — not even a remote font.

Permissions requested

Permission Why
storage Save your settings and the current session state.
scripting Activate the extension in tabs that were already open when it was installed, so you do not have to reload them.
http://*/* and https://*/* Catalogues live on countless shops; the scroll engine must run on the page you are browsing. It reads the page structure to find the "next page" link, and nothing else.

What Lèche never does

  • Click a payment, checkout or sign-in button.
  • Fill in a form, a password field or a captcha.
  • Dismiss or bypass a consent banner.
  • Read or transmit the content of the pages you visit.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or processed by the publisher, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. Everything the extension stores is under your control: uninstalling it erases that data, and the "Forget this site" button removes a learned site at any time.

Data related to your payment (email address, licence status) is processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. See their respective policies for that part.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new update date. A change that widened data collection would also be announced inside the extension itself.
