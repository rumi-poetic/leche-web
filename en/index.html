The feature that changes everything

A stubborn site? Teach it in two clicks.

No two shops paginate alike. Lèche recognises the vast majority on its own — and for the rest, you show it.

You point, it remembers

Click Learn this site in the panel, then click the site's own “next page” button. That's it. The teaching click is never passed on to the shop, so you cannot trigger anything by accident.

Before saving, Test highlights the element it picked and shows where it leads. From then on the site is recognised on every visit, and you can export your learned sites to carry them to another machine.

Lèche stops by itself on checkout flows and sign-in pages. It never clicks a payment button.

Learning mode: the chosen selector, testable before you save.

Questions

What people ask.

Which browsers?

Chrome, for now. Other browsers are not officially supported yet.

Which sites does it work on?

The vast majority of shops. Infinite scroll and “Load more” buttons are handled natively. Classic pagination is detected automatically. For sites that do it differently, learning mode settles the matter in two clicks, permanently.

What about my data?

Nothing is collected, nothing is sent. Your settings and learned sites stay in your browser. The only network exchange is the payment service checking your licence. Read the privacy policy.

Could it buy something on my behalf?

No. Lèche stops on checkout, cart and sign-in pages, and refuses to click any button whose label suggests a payment. The learning-mode click is never passed on to the site.

If I change computer, do I pay again?

No. Your licence is tied to your email address, not to the machine. You can also export your learned sites and import them elsewhere.
