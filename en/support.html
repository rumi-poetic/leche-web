What helps us answer quickly

  • The address of the site concerned.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The extension version, shown at the bottom of the panel.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visible.

The most common cases

The site will not move to the next page

Use learning mode: click “Learn this site” in the panel, then click the site's own next-page button. It is remembered permanently. See the walkthrough.

The page does not scroll at all

Some sites scroll an inner frame rather than the page. Lèche detects it after two seconds. If nothing moves, the panel says so — write to us with the address of the site.

The extension stays locked after payment

Reload the page. If it persists, write to us from the email address used at purchase and we will restore access.

I want a refund

Fourteen days, no questions asked. Write to us with the purchase email address. See the terms of sale.

I am changing computer

Your licence follows your email address. Reinstall the extension and it unlocks. For your learned sites, use “Export my sites” then “Import”.
