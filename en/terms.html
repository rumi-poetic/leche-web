Behaviour and limits

Lèche scrolls pages and, if you enable it, moves to the next page. Automatic pagination detection works on the vast majority of shops, and learning mode covers the rest. We do not, however, guarantee that it works on any particular site: shops change their structure without notice.

The extension does not work on the browser's internal pages, on the Chrome Web Store, or on local files — that is a browser restriction.

Lèche never makes a purchase, never fills in a form, and stops by itself on checkout and sign-in pages.
